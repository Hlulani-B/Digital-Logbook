OAuth redirects must land at this module's callback route; `AuthCallback` expects either a hash fragment containing `access_token`+`refresh_token` or a query `code` parameter from Supabase. Soft-deleted account restoration is triggered by passing `restore_email` and `restore_scheduled_at` query params into `/signin`, which clears them after reading and polls `checkUser` every 5 seconds until the account is restored.
