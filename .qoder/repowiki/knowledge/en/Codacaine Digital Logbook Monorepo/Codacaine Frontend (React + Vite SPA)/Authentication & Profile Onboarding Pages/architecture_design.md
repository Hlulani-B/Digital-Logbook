Three sibling page components form the authentication entry flow: `SignIn.tsx` is the main entry point offering email/password and Google/GitHub OAuth flows, plus a soft-deleted account restore prompt; it delegates credential/OAuth calls to the shared `useAuth()` context and routes via `routeAfterAuth`, which consults `checkUser` from `functions/profile/login.js` to decide between `/dashboard`, `/create-profile`, or the restore UI. `AuthCallback.tsx` is the OAuth redirect handler that parses hash-based tokens (implicit flow) or PKCE `code` parameters from Supabase, exchanges them for a session via `getSupabase().auth`, stores the email in `localStorage`, then reuses `checkUser` to route existing vs. new vs. soft-deleted accounts. `CreateProfile.tsx` completes onboarding by calling `addEmail`, `updateName`, and `updateUsername` from `functions/profile/profile.js` before navigating to `/avatar`. Dependency direction is one-way: pages depend on `@/context/AuthContext`, `@/lib/supabase`, `@/lib/validation`, and `../functions/profile/*`; there are no cross-imports between these three pages.
